NOS-Pipe 1.0.2 — project credits
================================

NOS-Pipe was developed from the ideas and open source of notPipe and JTube,
then substantially adapted for desktop Java 5, legacy computers, MPlayer,
multiple gateways, unusual display sizes and keyboard-only operation.

notPipe by Gohoski:
  https://github.com/gohoski/notPipe
  notPipe is published under WTFPL version 2. Its separately credited NNJSON
  component is published under the MIT License.

JTube:
  based in part on the open JTube-master source archive retained with the
  NOS-Pipe development history.

NOS-Pipe is not an official Google/YouTube client.
